Guides

Plain Guides for the Christian Life

How to pray, read the Bible, keep the Lord’s Day, share the gospel, and examine yourself. How to test a teacher, a doctrine, and a church. And what the Bible says about marriage, sex, children, work, and the hard questions.

Before you begin

What These Are

Short, practical guides for things every Christian has to do or decide. Each one is meant to be read in a few minutes and used the same day.

They are not a new rule. Scripture is the rule, and the Westminster Confession and Catechisms are the summary of it this site holds. These guides only put that teaching into plain steps. Where a guide says more than Scripture says, trust Scripture and leave the guide.

None of them replaces your elders. Take hard questions to the men Christ set over you. If you have no church yet, start with How to Find a Faithful Church.

Part one

The Christian Life

Prayer, the Word, the law of God, worship as God commands it, the Lord’s Day, looking honestly at yourself, and telling others about Christ.

  1. How to PrayWhat prayer is, how to pray through Christ, the Lord’s Prayer as a pattern, and the habits that keep a believer praying.
  2. How to Read the BibleHow to read Scripture with understanding, where to start, and which helps are worth having.
  3. How to Examine YourselfQuestions from Scripture and the Larger Catechism to ask yourself, how to do it each day, and how not to drown in it.
  4. The Ten Commandments for a BelieverWhy a forgiven Christian still keeps God’s law, how to read it, what each commandment requires and forbids, and how to use them this week.
  5. How God Is to Be WorshipedThe regulative principle in plain words. God decides how he is worshiped, what he has commanded, what that rules out, and why it matters.
  6. How to Keep the Lord’s DayWhy God set apart one day in seven, how to keep it, what counts as necessity and mercy, and how to make it a delight for children.
  7. How to Lead Family WorshipRead, sing, and pray. A few minutes a day, the way Reformed households have done it for centuries.
  8. How to Share the GospelKnow the message, pray for people by name, tell the bad news and the good, call for repentance and faith, and bring them to church.

Part two

Discernment and the Church

How to test a teacher, a doctrine, and a church against Scripture, and how to find a faithful congregation to belong to.

  1. How to Spot a False TeacherSeven questions to test any teacher by Scripture, the common warning signs today, and what to do when you find one.
  2. How to Spot Real HeresyThe difference between heresy, serious error, and honest disagreement, the truths that mark the line, and how to respond.
  3. How to Tell a True Church From a False OneThe marks of a true church, what makes a church false and why, the warning signs, and what to do.
  4. How to Find a Faithful ChurchHow to find a Reformed Presbyterian congregation, what to expect when you visit, what to do if none is near, and the questions to ask any church.

Part three

What the Bible Says

People often ask what the Bible says about this or that. These pages answer for the questions that come up most, at home and at work and in the world, including the hard ones. Each sets out what Scripture teaches and what the Westminster Standards draw from it. Each also has a word for the reader who is hurting or guilty, because God’s law always sends a sinner to Christ.

  1. What the Bible Says About MarriageGod made marriage before sin entered the world. What it is, what it pictures, what husbands and wives owe each other, and when it may end.
  2. What the Bible Says About SexSex is a good gift of God, made for marriage. What Scripture gives, what it forbids, and the cleansing Christ gives the guilty.
  3. What the Bible Says About Homosexuality and GenderGod made us male and female. What Scripture says, plainly and kindly, to the curious, the struggling, and the families who love them.
  4. What the Bible Says About Raising ChildrenChildren are a heritage of the Lord. Teaching them, disciplining them, praying for them, and walking with them through the teen years.
  5. What the Bible Says About Spanking and DisciplineWhat the rod in Proverbs means, how to correct a child in love and never in anger, and where discipline ends and abuse begins.
  6. What the Bible Says About AbortionThe unborn child bears the image of God. What Scripture says, the hard cases, help for a mother who is afraid, and mercy for the guilty.
  7. What the Bible Says About WorkGod gave work before the fall. Working as to the Lord, a hard boss, laziness and overwork, providing for a family, and resting in Christ.
