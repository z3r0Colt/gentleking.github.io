About these guides

Before you begin

About These Guides

Practical teaching for things Christians have to do or decide. Some pages give a brief answer. Others take time to work through. Choose what you need today.

They aren’t a new rule. Scripture is the rule, and the Westminster Confession and Catechisms are the summary of it this site holds. These guides only put that teaching into plain steps. Where a guide says more than Scripture says, trust Scripture and leave the guide.

Each guide stands on its own. Browse by topic, or use the search to find one answer.

None of them replaces your elders. Take hard questions to the men Christ set over you. If you have no church yet, start with How to Find a Faithful Church.

Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.
