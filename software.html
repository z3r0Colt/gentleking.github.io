Software

Gentle King Study

A Bible study application being built to get out of the way and put you in the text. It is still in development, so there is nothing to download yet.

The flagship project

Gentle King Study

Gentle King Study is a desktop Bible study application being built for one purpose, to set the text of Scripture in front of you and then stay out of your way.

In development

There is no shortage of Bible software, and much of it is good. But a great deal of it now sits behind a monthly subscription, so the man who can least afford it gets the least help. Other programs bury the verse under panels and pop-ups until the smallest thing on the screen is the word of God. Some of it is built the way a news feed is built, to keep you clicking and moving, when what your soul needed was to sit in one chapter long enough for that chapter to sit in you.

This program is being built the other direction. Large text. Quiet margins. Helps that come when you reach for them and disappear when you do not. Nothing on the screen is there to sell you anything.

Scripture is not a database to be queried. It is the living voice of God, and it carries you to a Person. On the road to Emmaus our Lord opened the Scriptures to two of his own disciples and showed them himself in them.

And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he interpreted to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself.

Luke 24:27 (ESV)

An honest word about where things stand. The application is unfinished. There is nothing to download and no release date. No promises about what week or what month either. The work gets done in the hours left over after a job and a family, which means it moves slowly. It will be posted here when it is ready and not one day sooner.

Design intent

What It Is Meant to Do

Nothing below is a shipped feature. These are the things the program is being built to do, described so you can judge whether it would be of any use to you. Some may change. Some may be cut.

The Text, Uncluttered

The whole point

Open the program and you should see a chapter, readable and unhurried, with no banner asking you to upgrade. Everything else is meant to fold away.

Notes That Stay Yours

Stored on your own machine

What you write about a passage belongs to you. The aim is plain files kept locally, which you can back up or carry off to another program if you ever want to.

Cross References and Parallels

Scripture interpreting Scripture

A verse should open into the rest of the Bible. Where one Gospel runs alongside another, or a psalm is taken up by an apostle, the goal is to set them side by side without losing your place.

Help With the Original Words

For readers with no Greek or Hebrew

Most of us never learned the languages. A word study ought to be within reach anyway, offered in a way that guards a reader from drawing more out of a word than the sentence will bear.

The Confessions Kept Close

Westminster Confession and Catechisms

The church did not begin reading her Bible yesterday. The intent is to have the Confession and the two Catechisms a keystroke away, with their proof texts, so you can weigh what you are seeing.

Plans for Reading and Memory

Daily use

Some want to read the whole Bible in a year. Others want to hide ten verses in the heart and keep them there. Both are being planned for, without badges or streaks to shame you.

A Place for Sermon Notes

The Lord’s Day and after

Preaching is a means of grace, and notes taken under it deserve better than a shoebox. Notes should attach to the passage preached, so that years later you can find what you heard.

Working With the Network Off

Desktop, not a web page

Your Bible should not stop working when the internet does. The design assumes the text, your notes, and the study helps all live on the machine in front of you.

Why it is built this way

The Convictions Behind It

Scripture is the authority. The tool is a servant, and a servant can be dismissed. If the program ever makes itself more interesting than the text, it has failed, however clever the code was.

No software has ever made a man a Christian. Charts, maps, parsing tables, and a thousand cross references can sit in front of a dead heart and leave it dead. The Holy Spirit alone gives life, and he gives it freely, before we are able to believe. Then he works by his word, read and preached, to bring that new life to faith in Christ.

So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

Romans 10:17 (ESV)

So this is offered as a help to the ordinary means of grace, never as a substitute for them. It will not sit you under preaching. It will not carry you to the Lord’s Table. It will not pray for you.

Study without prayer puffs a man up. Knowledge that never becomes worship curdles. The safeguard is not less study but study on the knees, asking God to show you Christ in his own book.

Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15 (ESV)

That verse assumes work. Handling the word rightly takes time and care, and the Bereans set the pattern for the rest of us.

Now these Jews were more noble than those in Thessalonica; they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so.

Acts 17:11 (ESV)

They tested an apostle by the Bible. You should test this program, and the man writing it, the same way.

All things in Scripture are not alike plain in themselves, nor alike clear unto all; yet those things which are necessary to be known, believed, and observed, for salvation, are so clearly propounded and opened in some place of Scripture or other, that not only the learned, but the unlearned, in a due use of the ordinary means, may attain unto a sufficient understanding of them.

Westminster Confession of Faith 1.7

A Bible you actually read will do more for you than a library you only own.

Availability

There Is Nothing to Download Yet

No file exists for you to install. When the program is ready, it will be posted on this page, and the page will say what it is and what it is not. Until that day, any link claiming to be this software did not come from here.

If you would like to hear about it when it is finished, write and ask, and you will be told. No newsletter and no sales pitch. Just a note when there is something real to hand you.

Ask to be told when it is ready See the forthcoming free books

In the meantime, you do not need this program in order to meet the King it was named for. You need Christ himself. His word calls you to turn from your sin and trust him, and he turns no one away who comes. He is not a King who tolerates sinners at a distance. He calls them to himself, and he is gentle with them when they come.

Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.

Matthew 11:29 (ESV)

If you have never come to him, start at the gospel page rather than waiting on software.

Also in the works

Other Tools

Smaller programs get written along the way, usually because something in ordinary Christian life proved harder than it needed to be. As they are finished they will be posted here, free, with the same honesty about what they do and do not do. Two are sketched out so far.

A Memory Helper

Planned

A small program for hiding Scripture in the heart, built around review that returns at sensible intervals. Nothing is written yet beyond notes and a rough plan.

A Simple Sermon Note Tool

Planned

Something light enough to open during a service and clear enough to search years later, for those who do not want the whole study application just to take notes.

Both are planned. Neither is released, and no date is being promised for either one. If that changes, this page changes with it.
