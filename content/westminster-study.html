The Original 1647 Westminster Confession

All thirty-three chapters, with direct access to the historical wording and its Scripture proofs.

This index uses the original Westminster text, without the American revisions. The chapter links open Bible Reading Helps at the relevant chapter. That companion displays the original historical text and its proofs, with a link to The Westminster Standard, the sole source used for the Standards on Gentle King.

Each chapter is paired with an existing article that helps explain its subject. Some articles address a broad theme rather than every paragraph of the chapter. Read the Confession and Scripture for the full treatment. Scripture is the supreme and infallible rule of faith.

Why the Edition Matters

The original Confession's chapters 20, 23, and 31 teach duties concerning Christian liberty, the magistrate, and synods that were changed in later American editions. Gentle King follows the original text. When reading a later denominational constitution or historical commentary, identify its edition rather than assuming every paragraph is identical.

For an explanation of the relationship between Scripture and confessional subscription, see The Westminster Standards.
