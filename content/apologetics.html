Before you begin

Choose Your Question

Choose the question you want to examine. If you doubt Christianity, the articles below address common objections. If you trust Christ but fear you aren’t saved, the Assurance and Doubt pages address that concern.

But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear:

1 Peter 3:15 (KJV)

If you’re a Christian answering a friend, listen to the question and represent it fairly. Peter commands gentleness and reverence in giving an answer. Explain the reasons for your faith without treating the other person as an opponent to humiliate. Only the Spirit can give the new life the gospel promises.

For the One Who Objects

These articles reason from Scripture as God’s true Word. They examine evidence while also asking how our beliefs about God affect what we accept as evidence.

The Bible teaches that God reveals himself through creation and that people are responsible for their response to him. Our questions therefore concern worship and obedience as well as knowledge.

For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:

Romans 1:20 (KJV)

Paul says that people knew God, yet didn't glorify him or give thanks, and suppressed the truth (Romans 1:18-21). That describes humanity’s sinful response to revelation. It doesn’t excuse a Christian from answering a particular question carefully.

Begin with whichever question is yours, or read the articles in order. Consider their reasoning alongside the cited passages. The Christian answer includes our need for reconciliation with God through Christ, so the articles also explain the call to repent and believe.
