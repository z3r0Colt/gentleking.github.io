Before you begin

Choose Your Question

These pages are written for two readers who could hardly be further apart. One does not believe and has his reasons. The other believes and is afraid he does not.

If you are the first, the first part is yours. It takes the common objections one at a time and answers them straight. It will not flatter you, and it will not pretend the question is only about evidence.

If you are the second, the second part is yours, and the arguments will stay out of your way. Each fear has a page of its own, written for you.

But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear:

1 Peter 3:15 (KJV)

If you are a Christian who wants help answering a friend, borrow the first part. Peter asks for the answer to be given with meekness, and meekness is the word the King chose for himself. An argument can clear rubble out of the road, but only the Spirit gives life. So the aim is never to win. It is to see your friend at the feet of Christ.

Part one

For the One Who Objects

Most arguments about God treat him as a question you may settle at your leisure. The Bible treats you as a witness who already knows the answer.

The usual approach puts God on trial and seats you in the jury box. That picture is false. No one comes to the question of God neutral. You come already sure of what is possible and what could count as proof, and so do I.

For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:

Romans 1:20 (KJV)

Paul says that men knew God, yet did not glorify him as God and were not thankful, and so they held the truth down (Romans 1:18-21). Unbelief is not a lack of light. It is a quarrel with it.

So where your objection is about facts, you will get facts, and only ones that hold. Where it goes deeper, you will see that you must borrow from God to make it at all. And these pages will keep speaking to your conscience, because the trouble between you and God is moral before it is intellectual. They are meant to be read in order, but begin with whichever question is yours.
