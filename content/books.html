Everything Here Is Free

Every book on this page is given away. No price, no paywall, no cart.

The reason is simple. What I have to hand you was never mine to sell. God gave his own Son for sinners, and he gave him freely, to people who had nothing to trade. Grace with a price tag on it stops being grace.

“Come, everyone who thirsts, come to the waters; and he who has no money, come, buy and eat! Come, buy wine and milk without money and without price.”

Isaiah 55:1 (ESV)

Jesus said much the same thing when he sent his disciples out into the villages.

“Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. You received without paying; give without pay.”

Matthew 10:8 (ESV)

I am not an apostle, and these books are not Scripture. They are the work of an ordinary man who reads his Bible, pushes a broom for a living, loves his wife and children, and writes down what he finds. Still, the principle holds. If the Lord gives light, it goes out the door free.

In Progress

What I Am Working On

Three books are underway. None of them is finished, and no files are posted yet. Here is honestly what each one is meant to be, so you know what is coming.

Sure of Him

Colt · Gospel and assurance

A book for the believer who keeps checking his own pulse and finding it weak. It works through what Christ actually did, and why his righteousness is credited to the one who trusts him. It tells a shaking soul where to look for rest. Your standing before God rests on his obedience, not on the strength of your feelings.

Coming Free

Grace for Plain People

Colt · The doctrines of grace

The doctrines of grace, laid out for someone who has never read a systematic theology and has no plans to. Election, the cross, the call that raises a dead man to life, and the keeping power of God, in ordinary words with the texts open. Not a debate manual. A comfort.

Coming Free

A House That Prays

Colt · Family worship

On family worship and raising children in the fear of the Lord. Reading, singing, and praying together without pretending your home is quieter or holier than it is. A father who is still learning it wrote it, for fathers and mothers who want to start and do not know how.

Coming Free

All three are aimed at the same place. Not at making you a better reader, but at putting you in front of the King who is strong enough to save and gentle enough to be approached.

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.”

Matthew 11:28-29 (ESV)

And the third book leans hard on a command older than any parenting method.

“And these words that I command you today shall be on your heart. You shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise.”

Deuteronomy 6:6-7 (ESV)

When They Are Ready

How to Get Them

There is nothing to sign up for. When a book is done it will simply appear here.

When a book is finished, the file goes up right on this page. Nothing to buy, no email address required, no account to make. You click and it is yours.

If you would rather be told when one goes up, say so and I will let you know.

Ask to be told when one is posted

Permission

Copy Them, Print Them, Pass Them On

Once a book is posted, it is yours to spread. Print it. Run copies for a Sunday school class. Leave a stack on a church table. Read it out loud to your children. Send the file to a friend who is sitting in the dark. You do not need to write and ask me first. You have permission now.

I ask two things, and neither one is a legal threat. Do not sell them, and do not change the words while keeping my name on them. Quote a paragraph in a lesson or a letter if it helps. A mention of where it came from is kind, though I am not counting.

“The Spirit and the Bride say, ‘Come.’ And let the one who hears say, ‘Come.’ And let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life without price.”

Revelation 22:17 (ESV)

The water of life was never for sale, and neither is anything I have to say about it.
