If the thought of following them has come to you, you're not strange and you're not alone. Call 988 or text 988 now. It answers at any hour. Outside the United States, call your local emergency number.

This article is for someone mourning a suicide. If you’re in danger of harming yourself, use the immediate safety guidance and tell someone safe near you. Christ’s promise to keep his people must never be used to excuse self-harm.

Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee.

Psalm 139:12 (KJV)

God knows the person you lost more fully than anyone else did. There are things about their final hours you may never learn.

When Lazarus died, his sisters each told Jesus that their brother would have lived if he had been there (John 11:21, 32). Jesus saw their tears, was troubled, and wept (John 11:33-35). You may have painful questions about what you missed or should have said. Tell the Lord what you’re thinking, and speak with someone who can listen without pretending to answer every question.

Taking one’s own life is a grave sin against the sixth commandment (Exodus 20:13, Larger Catechism Q&A 136). Yet no sin committed in a believer’s final hour can undo Christ’s saving work. The Westminster Confession teaches that those truly united to him cannot totally or finally fall away from grace (17.1). We cannot determine whether a particular person belonged to Christ simply from the manner of their death.

For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 8:38-39 (KJV)

If the one you lost belonged to Christ, death couldn’t separate them from his love. He died for his sheep and rose again. His promise that no one can pluck them out of his hand remains sure (John 10:28-29). This is a promise to believers, not a way to know the secret condition of another person’s heart.

You may have heard that taking one’s own life is the unforgivable sin. It isn’t. Jesus named one sin that's never forgiven, blasphemy against the Holy Ghost. He spoke of it because men said he had an unclean spirit (Mark 3:28-30).

The devil once misused a promise of God to tempt Jesus into danger, and Jesus refused to put God to the test (Matthew 4:6-7). Scripture never treats God’s preserving grace as permission to take one’s life.

If you fear the one you lost didn’t know Christ, that grief is real. You aren’t asked to settle where they are, and your fear doesn’t get the last word. Leave them with the Judge of all the earth, who will do right (Genesis 18:25), and bring your own soul to Christ, who turns away no one who comes. When You Fear They Did Not Know Christ says more.

If the death has just happened, ask a trustworthy person to stay close and help with the immediate calls. You don’t have to tell the whole story to everyone who asks.

Questions You May Be Asked

People sometimes ask why it happened, whether you saw warning signs, or what the person’s last words were. You can say that you don’t know or that you aren’t ready to speak. If there are children, give them a truthful explanation suited to their age, without graphic details or blame. Ask a trusted adult for help with that conversation if you need one.

Your loved one’s choice isn’t yours to carry as though you made it. If you truly wronged them, confess your own sin to God. Grief may include anger as well as love, and you can tell a pastor or counselor about both.

In the Weeks That Follow

Some families need time to arrange a funeral or deal with investigators. Others find the days after the service harder than the first days. Tell one or two people what kind of company or help you would welcome.

If you witnessed the death or keep reliving what happened, seek medical or trauma-informed counseling. If thoughts of ending your own life appear, tell someone nearby and call 988 or text 988 in the United States.
