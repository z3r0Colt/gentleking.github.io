What Are You Facing?

If someone you love has died, your home has changed, or you don’t know how to face tomorrow, begin with the trouble you’re carrying. Each article below brings Scripture and counsel to that particular sorrow.

Need urgent help? If you’re in immediate danger, call 911. If you’re thinking of suicide, call 988 or text 988 in the United States, and tell someone safe near you.

Someone hurting you? Call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788. See the safety page if your device may be monitored. Outside the United States, contact local emergency services.

The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

A contrite spirit is one brought low before God. This psalm teaches that the Lord hears those who cry to him. Jesus announced in the synagogue at Nazareth that he came to heal the brokenhearted (Luke 4:18, 21).

The Savior

Christ Knows Grief Firsthand

The Son of God took our nature and knew sorrow during his earthly life.

He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not. Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did esteem him stricken, smitten of God, and afflicted.

Isaiah 53:3-4 (KJV)

Jesus wept at Lazarus’s grave, suffered betrayal, and prayed in agony before his arrest. On the cross he cried, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? (Matthew 27:46). As the incarnate Son, he truly suffered in his human nature and bore the judgment due to his people’s sins. The Trinity wasn’t divided, and his divine nature wasn’t separated from the Father. Christ rose on the third day. Through him, every believer has the promise, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee (Hebrews 13:5). The Larger Catechism teaches that he became man to sympathize with our infirmities and give us access to God (Q&A 39).

For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin. Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.

Hebrews 4:15-16 (KJV)

He remains a merciful high priest. Bring your sorrow to him.

Prayer

When You Can't Pray

Grief can take the words out of your mouth. God has provided for that too.

Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

Romans 8:26 (KJV)

Paul doesn’t require a grieving believer to find the right words. He says the Spirit helps our infirmities and makes intercession according to God’s will (Romans 8:26-27). Christ also intercedes for his people at the Father’s right hand (Romans 8:34). Weakness in prayer doesn’t leave a believer without help.

When you can’t find words, read Psalm 13, 42, or 88. Psalm 88 ends without an easy resolution, but it begins by calling upon the God of salvation. In Psalm 56:8, David pictures God keeping his tears in a bottle. The picture teaches that the Lord knows the grief his people bring to him.
