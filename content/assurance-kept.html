For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 8:38-39 (KJV)

Paul doesn’t say you're kept by your love for God. He says nothing can separate you from God’s love for you, and that love is in Christ Jesus our Lord, where no fear of yours can reach it. When Zion said the Lord had forgotten her, God answered through Isaiah.

Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? yea, they may forget, yet will I not forget thee. Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.

Isaiah 49:15-16 (KJV)

Moses, blessing Israel before his death, told them that the eternal God was their refuge and that underneath were the everlasting arms (Deuteronomy 33:27). On the nights you can’t hold on to anything, that’s still where you are.

God preserves his people by sustaining their faith and repentance through the means he has appointed (WCF 17.1-2 and 14.1). A believer may stumble badly, but the Lord won't leave him finally hardened and lost. His promises give strength to obey the commands to persevere.

Do these things while you wait for the fear to lift.

  1. Seek counsel about the Lord’s Table. Question 172 of the Larger Catechism says the Supper was appointed for the relief even of weak and doubting Christians. One who doubts should mourn his unbelief and labor to have his doubts resolved, and as he does, he may and ought to come, so that his faith is made stronger. Take your doubts to your elders. The counsel of question 172 is for those who mourn their unbelief and sincerely seek Christ, not those persisting without repentance. Self-examination and the church's oversight still matter (WLC 171-173).
  2. Keep sitting under the preached Word. God strengthens faith through his Word (WCF 14.1). Gather with a faithful congregation when you’re able. If illness or disability prevents attendance, ask for visits, prayer, and help hearing the Word. If you have no church, the church guide explains how to look for one.
  3. Pray Mark 9:24 honestly. Tell him you believe and ask him to help your unbelief. Ask again tomorrow.
  4. Learn a few places in Scripture you can find in the dark. Romans 8 answers the accuser. Psalm 103 speaks to shame, and John 10 to the fear that you will slip from his hand.

Isaiah 40 opens with God commanding comfort for his people. A few verses on, Isaiah sees the Lord God come with a strong hand, his arm ruling for him. The next thing that arm does is gather lambs.

He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.

Isaiah 40:11 (KJV)
