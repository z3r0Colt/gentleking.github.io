Before you begin

What These Are

Short, practical guides for things every Christian has to do or decide. Each one is meant to be read in a few minutes and used the same day.

They are not a new rule. Scripture is the rule, and the Westminster Confession and Catechisms are the summary of it this site holds. These guides only put that teaching into plain steps. Where a guide says more than Scripture says, trust Scripture and leave the guide.

Each part can be read in order, as a short course, and each guide also stands on its own when you need one answer.

None of them replaces your elders. Take hard questions to the men Christ set over you. If you have no church yet, start with How to Find a Faithful Church.

Part one

The Christian Life

Prayer, the Word and meditation on it, looking honestly at yourself, repentance, the law of God, worship and the preached Word, the Lord’s Supper and the Lord’s Day, fasting, forgiveness, and telling others about Christ.

Part two

Discernment and the Church

How to read Scripture and its prophecy rightly, and how to test a teacher, a doctrine, a claimed word from God, and what comes through your screen. How to disagree with a brother, when to leave a church, and how to find a faithful one.

Part three

What the Bible Says

People often ask what the Bible says about this or that. These pages answer for the questions that come up most, at home and at work and in the world, including the hard ones. Each sets out what Scripture teaches and what the Westminster Standards draw from it. Each also has a word for the reader who is hurting or guilty, because God’s law always sends a sinner to Christ.
