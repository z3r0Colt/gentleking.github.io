Why Doctrine Isn't the Enemy of Devotion

Start Here

Doctrine is simply what you believe about God. You already have some. The only real question is whether it’s true.

You can't speak of Jesus without saying who he is, what he has done, and why he came. Those are doctrines. You've said he is real. You've said something about who he is and what he has done. The man who says God is love is teaching doctrine. So is the man who says God would never send anyone to hell. Nobody stands outside of doctrine. We only stand inside good doctrine or bad.

There's a real danger here, and it should be named. A person can know the terms and never know the Lord. He can argue well and pray little. That’s a sad thing, and Scripture warns about it. But the cure for cold knowledge isn’t less knowledge. It’s knowledge that reaches the heart. You can’t love a God you’ve refused to learn anything about. You can’t trust a Savior whose work you’ve never understood. The doctrines on this page aren’t a cage around Christ. They’re windows. Each one lets more light in on his face.

If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.

John 8:31-32 (KJV)

So read slowly. Look up the references. Ask God to show you himself in it.
