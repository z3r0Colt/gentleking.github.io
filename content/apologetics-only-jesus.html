All religions lead to God

They can’t all lead to the same place, because they don’t agree about where it is. Islam teaches that Jesus wasn't crucified, though almost every historian holds that he was. Christianity puts his cross at the center of everything. To say all religions are the same is to say each is wrong about what it cares about most. That claim dismisses the central teachings of the religions it claims to respect.

Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

John 14:6 (KJV)

Only one faith says that God became a man and died for the sins of his enemies. If there were another way, the cross would be the most needless cruelty in history. The night before he died, Jesus asked his Father, if it were possible, to let the cup pass from him (Matthew 26:39). It didn’t pass, because there was no other way.

What about those who never heard?

No one will be condemned for rejecting a Christ he never heard of. Men are judged for sinning against the light they had, in creation and in conscience (Romans 1:20, 2:12-15). But ignorance saves no one. If it did, the kindest thing the church could do would be to keep quiet. Christ sent his church to every nation because men cannot call on one they have not heard of (Romans 10:14). Apart from him no one is saved, however carefully he lives by the light of nature or his own religion (WCF 10.4).

You've heard the gospel. Christ commands you to repent and believe now.

It's arrogant to say you're right

Every claim to truth rules out its opposite, including the claim that Christians are wrong. Arrogance lies in how a man holds what he believes and why he thinks he came by it. We didn’t find God by being wiser or better than anyone. We were lost, and he found us, and we have nothing we did not receive (1 Corinthians 4:7). A man pulled out of the river isn’t boasting when he tells you where the rope is.

Consider Christ's Claim

  1. Read John start to finish. Mark each time Jesus says I am. Ask whether a good teacher who wasn’t the only way could talk like that.
  2. Write down the religion or view you find most fair. Set down what it says about sin and how a person is put right with God. Then ask a pastor to compare it with the gospel.
  3. Go to a faithful church on Sunday and listen. Notice who gets the credit for salvation in what is sung and preached. Listen for the rope.
