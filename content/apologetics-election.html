If God chooses, how can he blame me?

Thou wilt say then unto me, Why doth he yet find fault? For who hath resisted his will? Nay but, O man, who art thou that repliest against God? Shall the thing formed say to him that formed it, Why hast thou made me thus?

Romans 9:19-20 (KJV)

Paul raised your objection himself, and he had to, because he taught that God chooses. No one would raise it if God’s choice only followed ours. His first answer is a rebuke. The thing formed doesn’t put its maker on trial.

Then see what the Bible never says, that a man is condemned for not being chosen. He is condemned for his sin (WCF 3.7), and no one ever sinned against his own will. Every sin you’ve committed, you committed because you wanted to. Election pushes no one into sin. It pulls some out who would have stayed in it gladly. God wrongs no one when he leaves a man to the sin he loves.

Then there is no point in trying

God’s decree about you is hidden, and you don’t need to know it before you come. What is revealed is his command to repent and believe, and his promise that Christ will cast out no one who comes (John 6:37). Nothing has ever kept a sinner from Christ but that he would not come (John 5:40).

The secret things belong unto the Lord our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.

Deuteronomy 29:29 (KJV)

The God who ordains the end ordains the means, and the means is the gospel, heard and believed. The gospel page says more about election and the sinner who wants to come.

Christ's Invitation

  1. Read John start to finish. Watch for chapter 6, where Jesus speaks of the Father giving and drawing, and in the same breath invites all who come. See how he holds the two together.
  2. Write down your objection and ask a pastor. Put the hardest form of it in one sentence. A Reformed pastor has heard it before and won’t brush it off.
  3. Go to a faithful church on Sunday and listen. Notice that the gospel is offered to everyone in the room. Take that offer as meant for you.
