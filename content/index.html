The project

Gentle King Study

A desktop Bible study application for people who want to work through the text carefully, at home, in the hours they actually have.

Most of us study the Bible in the cracks of an ordinary week. Early morning before the house wakes up. A lunch break. Twenty minutes after the dishes. The tools we reach for are scattered. A Bible in one hand, a notebook somewhere, a browser tab full of things you will never find again. Gentle King Study is being built to gather that work into one place and leave it there, on your own machine, where you can come back to it next year and still find it.

The aim is simple. Put the text first, keep the helps close, and stay out of the way. In development

Here is what it is being built to do.

  • Read the ESV with your own notes open beside it, so the study and the text never drift apart.
  • Follow a cross reference and come back without losing your place.
  • Original language helps written for people who never learned Greek or Hebrew, so a word study does not require a seminary degree.
  • The Westminster Confession, the Shorter and Larger Catechisms, and other historic confessions kept next to the passage rather than on a shelf.
  • Sermon notes and study notes that live in files you own, on your computer, that no company can take back.
  • Reading plans for people who want a path through the whole Bible and for people who want to sit in one book for a long while.

Careful reading is not a hobby for specialists. The Bereans were commended for exactly this, examining the Scriptures daily to see whether the preaching held up, and Luke records it in Acts 17:11. The light is already in the text. A good tool only helps you hold the lamp steady.

Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.

Psalm 119:105 (ESV)

This software is still being written. There is nothing to download yet, and no release date is being promised, because none is known.

First things

The Gospel

Before any of this is useful to you, there is something you need to hear about God, about yourself, and about his Son.

God made you. He is holy, and he is good, and everything you have came from his hand. He has the right to your whole life, your obedience, your worship, and your love. That is not a hard saying. It is the most reasonable thing in the world.

You have not given him that. Neither have I. We have gone our own way, kept our own counsel, and made small gods out of things we could manage. Scripture does not soften this and neither will we.

for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,

Romans 3:23 (ESV)

Sin is not a mistake to be excused. It is a debt, and God is just, and a just judge does not let debts go unpaid. The wages of sin is death, and beyond death there is a judgment and a wrath that is real. Hell is not a scare tactic invented by preachers. It is what God says is coming for sin that is never dealt with.

Then God did the one thing no one would have dared ask. He sent his own Son. Jesus Christ, true God and true man, lived the obedient life you owe and did not live. He went to a Roman cross and there he took the guilt of his people on himself, and the wrath that belonged to them fell on him instead. He was buried. On the third day he rose bodily from the grave, and he lives now, reigning at the Father’s right hand.

For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 Corinthians 5:21 (ESV)

Notice the exchange. Your sin counted to him. His righteousness counted to you. That is the whole ground on which a sinner stands before God, and there is no other. Not your church attendance, not your clean record, not your sincerity, not your resolve to do better. Christ, and Christ received by faith.

So the call goes out. Turn from your sin and trust him. Repentance and faith are not two payments you make. They are the empty hands God gives you so you can receive his Son. Stop defending yourself. Stop managing your guilt. Come to him as you are, today, and he will not turn you away.

All that the Father gives me will come to me, and whoever comes to me I will never cast out.

John 6:37 (ESV)

There is no sinner so far gone that Christ will refuse him, and no sinner so respectable that Christ is optional.

This offer is free and it is for you. If you are reading this, you are among those to whom Christ is offered. Come.

Where this site stands

What We Hold

Gentle King is confessionally Reformed and Presbyterian. Scripture alone is the final authority, and we read it with the church, not apart from her. The Westminster Confession of Faith and the Shorter and Larger Catechisms are held as a faithful summary of what the Bible teaches, and they are cited here plainly rather than kept in the background.

That means grace from first to last. God chose a people before the world was made, Christ actually accomplished their redemption at the cross, the Spirit raises them from spiritual death so that they repent and believe, and not one of them is lost. It means one covenant of grace running from Genesis to Revelation in two administrations. It means worship shaped by God’s own directions, and the ordinary means of grace, the word preached, the sacraments, and prayer, treated as the ordinary way Christians grow.

Justification is an act of God’s free grace, wherein he pardoneth all our sins, and accepteth us as righteous in his sight, only for the righteousness of Christ imputed to us, and received by faith alone.

Westminster Shorter Catechism Q&A 33

Good works follow that. They never hold it up.

The name

Why Gentle King

Zechariah 9:9 tells Zion to shout, because her king is coming, righteous and having salvation, and he arrives humble on a donkey. Kings of that age came on warhorses. This one did not. Isaiah says the same thing another way.

a bruised reed he will not break, and a faintly burning wick he will not quench; he will faithfully bring forth justice.

Isaiah 42:3 (ESV)

He is a real King with real authority. He reigns now, and he will judge the living and the dead. And this is how he speaks to sinners who are worn out and know it.

Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

Matthew 11:28-30 (ESV)

That is the whole hope of this site. The King who has every right to break you has come to carry you instead.
