# Gentle King doctrinal-regression screening register

**Date:** October 30, 2026
**Baseline:** `190637a029fe79f5752ec5443cf7bb78a01d4363`
**Compared against:** `main` at start of this audit
**Scope:** 122 authored `content/*.html` page fragments. Includes doctrine, gospel, apologetics, comfort, assurances, guides, entry pages, and software marketing pages. Shared partials, builder-owned copy, historical quotations, Bible Reading Helps source data and app repositories are separate outstanding scope.

**Method and limits.** Every one of the 122 current `content/*.html` pages was retrieved and screened for its headings, sections, substantive themes, scriptural duties and relevant practical or protective language. Pages with high doctrinal stakes, prior significant changes, or a suspected omission were examined more closely. GitHub's comparison between the baseline and present main reported 52 page fragments changed or newly added. Those changes were compared by the diff and focused contextual reading for theological loss. Some long source files were screened structurally and by selected passages, not verified word-by-word against every proof text. Accordingly **this is a complete inventory-wide regression screen, not a complete line-by-line theological certification**. An unchanged fragment is not proof that its original doctrine was flawless. A citation or command being present does not independently establish correct exegesis.

**Confirmed regression corrections.** The marital-duty imbalance (PR 77) and mistaken equivalent of non-food abstinence with religious fasting (PR 78) were corrected before this batch. This batch restores the personal duty of the sermon hearer to examine his own heart before criticizing a minister, and clarifies that the rods in Proverbs describe physical correction in the ancient text while retaining appropriate child protection and avoiding universal prescriptions. The two new corrections are subject to check and deployment validation.

**Positive-duty checks not showing the same regression.** WCF 21.6-8 household worship and Sabbath observance, the original WCF 23.3 magistrate responsibility, WCF 30 church censure, WLC 160 preaching reception, Romans 6 and WCF 19 law and gospel, election, particular atonement, justification by faith and effectual calling, church membership, biblical repentance and the free offer of Christ remain expressly taught where screened. None of these results rules out a mistaken proof, incomplete application or a more subtle omission.

**Open interpretive questions.** The parenting, bodily discipline and other difficult ethical guides need careful article-by-article proof and contextual review. Do not force a favorable verdict from simple word counts or revert safety protections on the theory that plainness demands harm.
