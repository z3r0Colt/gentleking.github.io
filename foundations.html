Begin With Christ

This path starts with the gospel and follows the main teachings of the original 1647 Westminster Standards. It's for someone learning the Christian faith, or returning to its foundations.

Begin with the gospel even if you aren't yet sure what you believe. The readings are meant to be done with a Bible open. You can take one at a time. Each step names its required articles, a passage of Scripture, and the relevant paragraph of the original Confession with its Scripture proofs.

The pathway can't replace the public preaching of the Word, the sacraments, or the care of your elders. Find a faithful congregation as you study. If Christ is calling you through his Word, don't postpone repentance and faith until you've finished these lessons.

Sixteen Lessons in Order

  1. 1. Hear the gospel

    Who Christ is and why sinners must come to him now.

    Read Hear the gospel.

    Scripture Mark 1:14-15 (KJV) and WCF 7.3.

  2. 2. The God who speaks

    Why Scripture is our only infallible rule.

    Read The God who speaks.

    Scripture 2 Timothy 3:14-17 (KJV) and WCF 1.4.

  3. 3. God, our Creator

    The Father, Son, and Holy Ghost, one living God.

    Read God, our Creator.

    Scripture Matthew 28:18-20 (KJV) and WCF 2.3.

  4. 4. The fall and our need

    What Adam's sin did and why we can't rescue ourselves.

    Read The fall and our need.

    Scripture Romans 5:12-19 (KJV) and WCF 6.3.

  5. 5. Christ our Mediator

    The person and work of our Prophet, Priest, and King.

    Read Christ our Mediator.

    Scripture Hebrews 7:23-28 (KJV) and WCF 8.2.

  6. 6. Repentance and faith

    Turning from sin and believing the freely offered Savior.

    Read Repentance and faith.

    Scripture Acts 2:37-41 (KJV) and WCF 15.1.

  7. 7. Justification

    Accepted for Christ's righteousness, received by faith.

    Read Justification.

    Scripture Romans 4:1-8 (KJV) and WCF 11.1.

  8. 8. Union and adoption

    Belonging to Christ and received into the Father's family.

    Read Union and adoption, then Adoption.

    Scripture Romans 8:14-17 (KJV) and WCF 12.1.

  9. 9. A holy life

    The Spirit's work in the ongoing fight against sin.

    Read A holy life.

    Scripture Galatians 5:16-25 (KJV) and WCF 13.1.

  10. 10. Baptism and the covenant

    Christ's sign, believing households, and the visible church.

    Read Baptism and the covenant.

    Scripture Acts 2:38-39 (KJV) and WCF 28.4.

  11. 11. Worship and the Lord's Day

    How the Lord gathers and nourishes his people.

    Read Worship and the Lord's Day, then Keeping the Lord's Day.

    Scripture Acts 2:41-42 (KJV) and WCF 21.1.

  12. 12. Joining a church

    Membership, elders, and caring for the congregation.

    Read Joining a church.

    Scripture Hebrews 10:23-25 (KJV) and WCF 25.2.

  13. 13. The Lord's Supper

    The spiritual nourishment Christ gives his people.

    Read The Lord's Supper.

    Scripture 1 Corinthians 11:23-29 (KJV) and WCF 29.7.

  14. 14. Assurance and perseverance

    Trusting the Savior through weakness and doubt.

    Read Am I Really Saved?, then Will God Keep Me to the End?.

    Scripture John 10:27-29 (KJV) and WCF 18.3.

  15. 15. Prayer, family, and work

    Daily devotion and service in the place God has assigned.

    Read Prayer, then Family Worship, then Work.

    Scripture Deuteronomy 6:4-9 (KJV) and WCF 21.6.

  16. 16. Christ's return

    The resurrection, judgment, and everlasting life.

    Read Christ's return.

    Scripture 1 Corinthians 15:20-28 (KJV) and WCF 33.1.

How to Use the Lessons

Read the Scripture passage in context before the assigned articles. Take notes on the text, especially where an explanation surprises you. Open the original Westminster paragraph and examine its proofs. Ask your pastor or an elder about anything you can't settle from Scripture.

Read each assigned article before going to the next lesson. The Foundations navigation on those articles keeps your place in the course. If an earlier doctrine becomes difficult, return to it. There's no account, progress score, or badge to earn. The purpose is to learn God's Word and obey him.

For a complete chapter-by-chapter view of the Confession, see the Westminster study index. For practical help finding a congregation, read How to Find a Faithful Church.
