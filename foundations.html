Begin With Christ

This path starts with the gospel and follows the main teachings of the original 1647 Westminster Standards. It is for someone learning the Christian faith, or returning to its foundations.

Begin with the gospel even if you aren't yet sure what you believe. The readings are meant to be done with a Bible open. You can take one at a time. Each step links to the complete article, a passage of Scripture, and the relevant paragraph of the original Confession with its Scripture proofs.

The pathway cannot replace the public preaching of the Word, the sacraments, or the care of your elders. Find a faithful congregation as you study. If Christ is calling you through his Word, don't postpone repentance and faith until you've finished these lessons.

Sixteen Lessons in Order

  1. 1. Hear the gospel

    Who Christ is and why sinners must come to him now.

    Read Mark 1:14-15 (KJV) and WCF 7.3.

  2. 2. The God who speaks

    Why Scripture is our only infallible rule.

    Read 2 Timothy 3:14-17 (KJV) and WCF 1.4.

  3. 3. God, our Creator

    The Father, Son, and Holy Ghost, one living God.

    Read Matthew 28:18-20 (KJV) and WCF 2.3.

  4. 4. The fall and our need

    What Adam's sin did and why we cannot rescue ourselves.

    Read Romans 5:12-19 (KJV) and WCF 6.3.

  5. 5. Christ our Mediator

    The person and work of our Prophet, Priest, and King.

    Read Hebrews 7:23-28 (KJV) and WCF 8.2.

  6. 6. Repentance and faith

    Turning from sin and believing the freely offered Savior.

    Read Acts 2:37-41 (KJV) and WCF 15.1.

  7. 7. Justification

    Accepted for Christ's righteousness, received by faith.

    Read Romans 4:1-8 (KJV) and WCF 11.1.

  8. 8. Union and adoption

    Belonging to Christ and received into the Father's family.

    Read Romans 8:14-17 (KJV) and WCF 12.1.

  9. 9. A holy life

    The Spirit's work in the ongoing fight against sin.

    Read Galatians 5:16-25 (KJV) and WCF 13.1.

  10. 10. Baptism and the covenant

    Christ's sign, believing households, and the visible church.

    Read Acts 2:38-39 (KJV) and WCF 28.4.

  11. 11. Worship and the Lord's Day

    How the Lord gathers and nourishes his people.

    Read Acts 2:41-42 (KJV) and WCF 21.1.

  12. 12. Joining a church

    Membership, elders, and caring for the congregation.

    Read Hebrews 10:23-25 (KJV) and WCF 25.2.

  13. 13. The Lord's Supper

    The spiritual nourishment Christ gives his people.

    Read 1 Corinthians 11:23-29 (KJV) and WCF 29.7.

  14. 14. Assurance and perseverance

    Trusting the Savior through weakness and doubt.

    Read John 10:27-29 (KJV) and WCF 18.3.

  15. 15. Prayer, family, and work

    Daily devotion and service in the place God has assigned.

    Read Deuteronomy 6:4-9 (KJV) and WCF 21.6.

  16. 16. Christ's return

    The resurrection, judgment, and everlasting life.

    Read 1 Corinthians 15:20-28 (KJV) and WCF 33.1.

How to Use the Lessons

Read the Scripture passage in context before the article. Take notes on the text, especially where an explanation surprises you. Open the original Westminster paragraph and examine its proofs. Ask your pastor or an elder about anything you cannot settle from Scripture.

If you've already read a lesson, go to the next. If an earlier doctrine becomes difficult, return to it. There is no account, progress score, or badge to earn. The purpose is to learn God's Word and obey him.

For a complete chapter-by-chapter view of the Confession, see the Westminster study index. For practical help finding a congregation, read How to Find a Faithful Church.
