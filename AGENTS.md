# AGENTS.md - Instructions for coding and editorial agents

These instructions apply to work in this repository. Read them before making changes. For the complete full-site editorial program, follow PLAN.md and track verified progress in EDITORIAL_AUDIT.md when that work begins.

## Repository and mission

This is Gentle King Ministry, a static Reformed Presbyterian teaching and resource website published at https://gentleking.org.

The guiding editorial rule is **practical first when practical help is the purpose, and full biblical teaching when doctrine is the purpose**.

Answer the reader's question plainly. Explain what the reader actually needs to know or do. Use Scripture to establish the relevant truth, duty, and promise. Do not add spiritual phrases, metaphors, or gospel summaries as padding.

## The whole-counsel check for every ethical and pastoral revision

Before approving an edit involving a biblical obligation, check four things against the relevant Scripture and original 1647 Westminster Standards.

1. **The positive command.** What does God require of the people addressed? State it plainly even when it may be unpopular or uncomfortable.
2. **The prohibited neglect or abuse.** What sins arise from refusing this duty or exercising authority unlawfully? Name both where the passage does.
3. **The lawful limits and competing duties.** Explain genuine exceptions, inability, safety, and jurisdiction only where actually relevant. Qualifications must illuminate the rule, not quietly replace it.
4. **The proportion of the finished page.** Would an ordinary person reading the page learn what to obey, as well as how to avoid doing wrong? If the page is dominated by caveats and never teaches the normal case, revise it. Do not create an opposite error by deleting necessary protections.

Use this especially for marital intimacy, duties of husbands and wives, parental correction and children's obedience, church discipline and membership, forgiveness, evangelism, fasting, work, money, worship and the Lord's Day. Treat an editorial claim that "biblical duties are unsafe" as a reason to check whether the duty has been distorted or the safety issue has been poorly presented, not as permission to remove either. Never assume the critic, pastor, husband, wife, parent, elder, or victim is automatically right; the text must govern.

 Do not strip necessary biblical or confessional teaching from a page whose purpose is theology.

The site should read like a thoughtful layman speaking to a person, not an AI-generated study guide or a collection of inspirational slogans.

Read, in order, AGENTS.md, EDITORIAL_STYLE.md, README.md, and the relevant part of PLAN.md before working on an editorial batch. Examine actual page source and nearby pages rather than editing from memory.

## Work assignments

When assigned the full practical-first site overhaul, **execute the complete PLAN.md**, not merely an audit or a revised plan. Discover the present repository inventory; read every authored page; edit each page requiring change; track each reviewed item; verify; publish in sensible batches. A prior revision does not exempt a page from inspection.

For a narrower request, follow the same standards but limit modifications to the requested scope. Do not unexpectedly rewrite the entire site as a side effect of a small fix. Do not claim full-site completion from partial work.

Use EDITORIAL_AUDIT.md as a truthful page-by-page status register during the full project. Each page or separately authored public resource must be accounted for, including reviewed items that needed no change. Record discoveries, factual questions, changes, verification, outstanding issues, and PRs. If the audit file does not exist, create it in the first execution batch; do not mark unseen content reviewed.

## Editorial judgment by page type

**Comfort, assurance, safeguarding, crisis, grief, illness, and pastoral help**

- Identify the actual trouble and its urgency. Put immediate safety, food, shelter, medical attention, or protection first where relevant.
- Give direct, realistic guidance and name the appropriate person or service to contact. Consider disability, financial need, lack of a supportive church, abuse, isolation, and other obstacles.
- Explain biblical hope, God's providence, repentance, and the church's care only where those points truly apply. Do not assume each hardship reveals a hidden sin or spiritual failure.
- Never make prayer an alternative to necessary medical, legal, crisis, or protective services.
- Never inject an identical prominent danger banner into every Comfort article. Keep urgent contacts on the Safety page and use brief, relevant directions elsewhere.
- When writing to someone bereaved by suicide, begin with their grief and questions. Explain the sixth commandment and perseverance accurately and in their proper context, without guessing a particular person's eternal state.
- When writing to a person contemplating their own suicide, begin with a humane response and immediate safety, including clear crisis contacts and plain words they can use to tell someone. Explain Christian hope without delivering a commandment lecture, implying that distress proves unbelief, or delaying professional help.

**Money, marriage, parenting, employment, family, prayer, worship, and Christian life**

Marital-intimacy guidance must state and explain the whole teaching of 1 Corinthians 7:3-5, including the equal duty of husband and wife, the instruction not to defraud one another, and the ordinarily mutual, time-limited nature of abstinence for prayer. Do not imply the marriage bed is entirely optional according to each day's desire, or give one spouse power to neglect the other indefinitely. Include the seventh-commandment duties of conjugal love from original Westminster Larger Catechism 138-139. Distinguish enduring neglect from pain, disability, trauma, illness, postpartum recovery and other genuine impediments. Biblical obligation never permits forced sex or threats. Do not infer a spouse's motives or sexual capacity merely from other activities. This guidance must remain substantial even on pages intended for readers with no abuse or safety concern.

- State the answer early and show how biblical duties work in ordinary circumstances.
- Make the practical steps specific to the subject, not a generic checklist repeated across guides.
- Address real decisions, exceptions, safety concerns, dependent care, obligations, and the limits of the advice.
- Give readers clear routes to more detailed supporting material without repeating entire sections.

**Gospel, doctrine, confessions, foundations, and apologetics**

- Depth is required. Retain precise doctrinal definitions, Scripture proofs, original confessional positions, and the free call to come to Christ.
- Explain unfamiliar terms and the context of texts in understandable language.
- Distinguish Scripture, confession, historical claims, interpretation, and inference.
- Do not impose a practical tip on every theological paragraph, replace doctrinal argument with moral encouragement, or presume every reader is regenerate.
- In apologetics and teacher evaluations, be accurate and fair about competing claims and verify attributions.

**Software, download instructions, and library/resource pages**

- Describe actual implemented features, supported platforms, requirements, privacy, pricing, limitations, installation steps, and support channels.
- Ground product claims in the specific software repository, release, or documented source when accessible. Do not invent or update product facts by inference.
- Avoid devotional filler in technical instructions. Do not change code or assets for a copy-only task without reason.

## Style and language

Follow EDITORIAL_STYLE.md in full.

- Use natural American English, contractions, and varied sentence structure. Prefer plain words with precise meaning.
- No em dashes. Avoid semicolons, ornate colons in prose, melodramatic phrasing, artificial oppositions, and piles of one-sentence paragraphs.
- Delete repetitive lead-ins, generic conclusions, duplicated spiritual appeals, obligatory three-part lists, and filler.
- Prefer concrete examples of the actual reader's problem. Maintain professional, humane, straightforward language.
- Do not mistake removing repetition for shortening everything. Some topics need more explanation.
- Do not remove necessary "may," "might," "we do not know," or other qualifications just to sound forceful.
- Do not add a spiritual application to every paragraph or Scripture proof to every trivial statement.
- Avoid repetitive restatements of the same theological point within an article or across closely linked articles unless a standalone reader genuinely needs it.
- Read the complete revised text aloud or review sentence by sentence. A regex scan is only an early warning.

## Theology and quotations

This website teaches in the tradition of the **original 1647 Westminster Standards** and Reformed Presbyterian practice. For Westminster text and Scripture proofs use https://thewestminsterstandard.org/ exclusively. Do not quietly substitute the 1788 American revisions or later denominational changes, particularly in WCF 20, 23, and 31.

Maintain scriptural inerrancy, the Trinity, Christ's deity and true humanity, penal substitution, particular redemption, unconditional election, effectual calling, salvation by grace through faith alone, repentance accompanying faith, perseverance, covenant theology, presbyterian church government, and the regulative principle of worship with exclusive psalmody. Keep the free, immediate gospel offer to every sinner who hears the Word.

Preserve the exact text of all authorized KJV Scripture quotations, historical quotations, and original Westminster extracts. Do not mechanically contract words in quoted material or change "I AM". When a source excerpt is incomplete, indicate that honestly. Correct an erroneous quote only after checking it against an authoritative source, and record the change. Use verify_quoted_text.py against the known starting revision when appropriate.

Interpret verses in literary and historical context. Do not turn biblical narratives into modern psychiatric diagnoses, financial formulas, guarantees of healing, automatic salvation assurances, or guesses about providence. Do not draw conclusions about a particular person's election or final state when Scripture has not revealed them.

No representations of Christ or any person of the Godhead are to be created or introduced.

## Safety and third-party factual claims

Give especially careful review to suicide and self-harm, domestic violence, child abuse, pregnancy loss, addiction, mental health, end-of-life care, debt, bankruptcy, and legal reporting obligations.

- Check crisis lines, service capabilities, and safety instructions against authoritative current sources.
- Distinguish urgent danger from non-emergency counsel.
- Do not imply that a hotline automatically reports abuse, that counselors have a universal reporting duty, or that any service provides privacy or assistance it does not offer.
- Avoid individual medical diagnoses and definitive legal conclusions.
- Distinguish moral principles from professional medical or legal decisions.
- Put immediate protective action first when a child or adult is in danger.
- If verification is unavailable, qualify the claim or document it for further verification. Never invent facts.

## Where to edit

The website's authored HTML pages are in content/. The common blocks are in content/partials/. build.py contains the page registry, shared shell, navigation, page titles, descriptions, metadata, structured-data copy, search-related material, and other generated text. The root-level page HTML files are generated artifacts served by GitHub Pages; they are not the source of truth.

Use the actual layout and instructions in README.md. Understand references and historical URL mappings in build.py and reference_links.py. Preserve old fragment links and public URLs unless there is an explicit redirect plan. Do not edit binary resources, historical works, original Bible text, or imported source material as an editorial shortcut.

Gospel material is also shared with biblehelps/assets/content.json; check_biblehelps.py enforces related consistency. Keep source text, integrity metadata, Bible reference links, the 66-book KJV corpus, and Bible Reading Helps functionality intact. Review UI copy in JavaScript and JSON when in scope but avoid changing their behavior incidentally.

Inspect the generated search index and visible metadata whenever source copy or titles change.

## Verification before a PR merges

From the repository root, run the checks below when the environment supports them:

    python3 build.py
    python3 check.py
    python3 check_biblehelps.py
    python3 audit_site.py
    python3 check_foundations.py
    python3 contractions_audit.py
    node --check biblehelps/assets/companion.js
    node --check assets/js/corrections.js

Also apply verify_quoted_text.py where relevant. Review the generated HTML rather than trusting only the content fragment. Run browser_smoke.py / the Browser smoke test workflow for layout or site-wide content changes when tooling is available.

Check representative desktop, tablet, and phone layouts, link destinations, headings, breadcrumbs, IDs and anchors, historic redirects, search behavior, and relevant accessibility affordances. Confirm any newly modified legal, medical, or service claims are grounded.

If a check fails, fix the underlying issue. Do not remove validation steps, silence warnings, or mark a check successful when it was skipped. If tooling is unavailable, say what was not run and why.

## Git and publication workflow

- Work on a descriptive branch from current main and keep commits and pull requests reviewable.
- Do not force-push, overwrite unrelated work, or delete user changes.
- Note the specific files and page classes revised, the proof and quote checks, and test results in the PR.
- Merge only after applicable checks pass and the content has been reviewed.
- On push to main, the GitHub Actions workflow runs build.py, checks the site, and commits regenerated published pages if needed.
- Verify the generated pages appear on main and check the final GitHub Pages deployment status. Merging source changes does not by itself prove the public update deployed.
- Record verified progress in EDITORIAL_AUDIT.md. Do not close out the complete project until the full acceptance criteria in PLAN.md are satisfied.
- Explain any blockers and unfinished coverage specifically. Never report a whole-site rewrite if only a sample or one batch was completed.

## Required result for the full overhaul

Every discovered authored page and public-facing copy source has been read and evaluated; each needed revision is made or explicitly justified; all important quotations are preserved and checked; biblical and original Westminster teaching remains accurate; practical help is direct and safe; shared content, navigation, search, metadata, and generated output remain consistent; all relevant validation and browser checks succeed; reviewed batches are merged; the final GitHub Pages deployment succeeds; and the page inventory reports truthful complete coverage.

The governing question is not "How much religious language can we remove?" It is "Does every sentence help this reader understand the subject, follow the biblical teaching, or carry out a necessary action?"
