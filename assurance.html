Assurance and Doubt

Assurance and Doubt

For those who trust Christ but fear they're not really his.

Begin Here

Start with Am I Really Saved?, or choose the question that troubles you most.

Assurance rests on God's promises in Christ, not on a passing feeling. But Scripture also warns against false confidence. These articles take both seriously, following Westminster Confession chapters 17 and 18. You'll find practical counsel here, but the first question is always what the Lord has said.

  1. Am I Really Saved?Come to Christ while your doubts remain. Scripture and the Westminster Standards explain assurance, weak faith, self-examination, and the fear of not being chosen.6 min read
  2. When the Same Sin Keeps Coming BackRemaining sin calls for repentance and help. It does not overturn the justification of those who trust Christ. Read how Scripture holds warning and pardon together.6 min read
  3. The Unpardonable Sin and Falling AwayRead the warnings about blasphemy and apostasy in their context. Christ calls you to come to him rather than settle every fear by checking your feelings.7 min read
  4. When God Feels FarA loss of comfort does not by itself prove that God has forsaken you. Seek pastoral care, attend to health needs, and read what Scripture says to troubled believers.8 min read
  5. Doubts and Unwanted ThoughtsDoubting an argument and experiencing an unwanted thought call for different kinds of help. Find biblical counsel and guidance on seeking a clinical assessment.4 min read
  6. Is God Punishing Me?Illness, loss, or a breaking marriage does not reveal a secret verdict against you. Read about fatherly discipline and the freedom from condemnation believers have in Christ.4 min read
  7. Afraid of DyingTell your care team and someone you trust what frightens you. Scripture explains Christ's victory over death and the resurrection believers await.4 min read
  8. Will God Keep Me to the End?Christ keeps his people through faith, repentance, and the means he gives. His promise sustains obedience while believers seek assurance.3 min read

Matthew 11:29 (KJV)
