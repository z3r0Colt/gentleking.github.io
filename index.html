First things

The Gospel

There is no better news in all the world. To see why, you need to hear something about God and about his Son. And something about yourself.

God made you. He is holy, and he is good, and everything you have came from his hand. He has the right to your whole life. That is not a hard saying. It is the most reasonable thing in the world.

You have not given him that. Neither have I. We have gone our own way and made small gods out of things we could manage. Scripture does not soften this and neither will we.

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;

Romans 3:23 (KJV)

Sin is not a mistake to be excused. It is a debt, and a just judge does not let debts go unpaid. The wages of sin is death, and beyond death there is a judgment and a wrath that is real. Hell is not a scare tactic invented by preachers. It is what God says is coming on every sinner who dies outside of Christ.

Then God did the one thing no one would have dared ask. He sent his own Son. Jesus Christ, true God and true man, lived the obedient life you owe and did not live. He went to a Roman cross and there he took the guilt of his people on himself, and the wrath that belonged to them fell on him instead. He was buried. On the third day he rose bodily from the grave, and he lives now, reigning at the Father’s right hand.

For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.

2 Corinthians 5:21 (KJV)

Our sin counted to him. His righteousness counted to us. A sinner stands before God on that ground and no other. Not your church attendance, and not your resolve to do better. Christ, and Christ received by faith. The gospel page sets out how that righteousness is credited to a man.

So the call goes out. Turn from your sin and trust him. Repentance and faith are not two payments you make. They are gifts. God gives repentance so you will turn from your sin, and faith so you can take hold of his Son. Stop defending yourself. Stop managing your guilt. Come to him as you are, today, and he will not turn you away.

All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.

John 6:37 (KJV)

There is no sinner so far gone that Christ will refuse him, and no sinner so respectable that Christ is optional.

This offer is free and it is for you. If you are reading this, you are among those to whom Christ is offered. Come.

The name

Why Gentle King

Conquering kings came on warhorses. Jesus rode into Jerusalem on a donkey (Zechariah 9:9, Matthew 21:5). He has all authority, and he will judge the living and the dead. Yet he says he is meek and lowly in heart, and newer Bibles render that word gentle.

A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.

Isaiah 42:3 (KJV)

This site is confessionally Reformed and Presbyterian, and it holds the Westminster Confession and Catechisms. It is one layman’s work. About this site says who writes it and what it believes.

Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.

Matthew 11:29 (KJV)
