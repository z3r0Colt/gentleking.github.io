About This Site

Conquering kings came on warhorses. Jesus rode into Jerusalem on a donkey (Zechariah 9:9, Matthew 21:5). He has all authority, and he will judge the living and the dead. Yet he says he is meek and lowly in heart, and newer Bibles translate that word gentle.

A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.

Isaiah 42:3 (KJV)

This site is confessionally Reformed and Presbyterian, and it holds the Westminster Confession and Catechisms. It’s one layman’s work. About this site says who writes it and what it believes.

Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.

Matthew 11:29 (KJV)
